FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Who is Verity for?

Verity is built for anyone who needs a reliable record of their expenses — from contractors and freelancers to small businesses and teams managing employee purchases and reimbursements.

How does Verity handle reimbursements?

Reimbursements are created directly from existing expense records. The record can be shared for review, so the receipt, expense details, and supporting documents stay connected to the request instead of being copied into another system.

Can I share an expense with someone else?

Yes. Share individual records with collaborators when you need another person to review or work on an expense. Your other financial records remain private, so collaboration doesn't require handing over your entire expense history.

How does Verity differ from standard expense managers?

Verity treats an expense as more than a receipt. It connects the original purchase with bank transactions, supporting documents, warranties, reimbursements, sharing, and its history — giving you one place to understand the complete expense.

Is my financial data secure?

Verity is designed around keeping your financial records private. Documents and sensitive data are protected in transit and at rest, and shared access is limited to the records you choose to collaborate on.
