Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Papertrail and how it transforms purchase records.

How does Papertrail differ from standard receipt scanners?

Papertrail prioritizes simple P2P reimbursements. Generate detailed reimbursement requests to recipients effortlessly. Papertrail creates an entire digital workspace and evidence folder for every purchase, unifying all supporting documents into a searchable immutable record.

Is my financial data secure?

Papertrail is built with a security-first design philosophy. Financial credentials are encrypted, documents are uploaded via secure signed URLs directly to cloud storage, and access control is strictly enforced based on individual user ownership.

Can I export my data for accounting or tax purposes?

You can download all or select records as Excel reports. Export individual or bulk purchases, and keep organized records that fulfill 7-year document retention requirements effortlessly.
