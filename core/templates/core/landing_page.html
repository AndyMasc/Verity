01 / Process

A single continuous line from purchase to ledger.

Phase 01 — Capture

Instant Extraction

Drop any receipt, invoice, or billing document. Verity instantly extracts merchant metadata, itemized breakdown, tax codes, and amounts in ~9 seconds—eliminating manual typing completely.

Phase 02 — Match

Bank Transaction Link

Verity compares cleared bank transactions with your uploaded evidence automatically. Timestamps, totals, and merchant descriptors are verified in the background.

Frequently asked questions.

Verity is built for anyone who needs a reliable record of their expenses — from contractors and freelancers to small businesses and teams managing employee purchases and reimbursements.
Reimbursements are created directly from existing expense records. The record can be shared for review, so the receipt, expense details, and supporting documents stay connected to the request instead of being copied into another system.
