How does Papertrail differ from standard receipt scanners?

Unlike single-purpose scanning utilities, Papertrail acts as a complete asset dossier and financial clearinghouse—linking bank feeds, warranty records, tax compliance, and client settlements into dynamic workspaces.

Is my financial data secure across international jurisdictions?

Yes. All credentials and documents are protected with enterprise-grade encryption. Data is stored in secure, geographically isolated serverless storage buckets with strict cryptographic access limits.
